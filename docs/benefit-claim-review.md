## Why these lines and not the others

A catalog benefit that describes a sensation — "Releases the back and hips" —
is a description of the shape. A benefit that names a condition or a
physiological mechanism — "Eases mild anxiety and insomnia", "Calms the nervous
system", "Aids digestion" — asserts an effect. Only the second kind needs a
qualified reviewer, so only the second kind is listed.

While a line is on this list, the pose detail page shows a standing caveat
under its Benefits (`BENEFIT_REVIEW_NOTE`).

## What a reviewer decides

For each line, one of:

1. **Keep as written** — the claim is supportable and the wording is not stronger
   than the support. Record who decided and on what basis.
2. **Reword to a sensation** — e.g. "Calms the nervous system" becomes a
   description of what the shape does to the breath and the body.
3. **Remove.**

Editing the catalog changes the count pinned in
`client/src/lib/benefitClaims.test.ts`, which is the point: a new clinical claim
cannot be added without someone noticing they added one.
